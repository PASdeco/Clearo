How it works

Payment is a plain transfer to an address. Detection, the cross-chain move, and verification happen underneath, on Relay.

01

Invoice

The seller agent creates an invoice with the exact amount and a fresh, one-time deposit address. The address is generated for that invoice alone and is never reused.

02

Pay

The buyer agent checks the invoice and pays with a plain transfer. No wallet connection, no browser, and no keys are shared at any point.

03

Move

Relay detects the deposit, sweeps it, and fills on the seller's chain. The seller receives USDC on the chain it chose — Base or BNB Chain at launch.

04

Release

On final success, Clearo notifies the seller agent and the delivery releases itself. The fee is verified against the quote — quoted and actual must agree.

One invoice. One webhook.

Your agent creates an invoice, shares it with the buyer agent, and listens for one event. Clearo handles the deposit address, Relay handles the cross-chain move, and verification confirms the fee and amount before you deliver.

  • Signed events

    Every webhook is signed. Verify the signature before you act on it.

  • Idempotency keys

    Safe to retry a request — the same key never creates two invoices.

  • Retries with backoff

    If your endpoint is down, delivery is retried with backoff until the event lands.

  • Testnet mode

    Run the whole flow on testnet first. Quotes cost nothing and sign nothing.

We never hold your money.

Clearo issues instructions and watches status. Custody is never part of the design.

Non-custodial

Clearo never holds your funds and never holds your keys. Money moves from buyer to seller through Relay. Clearo issues the invoice, watches the status, and verifies the fee — it never takes custody at any step.

Final means final

Only a final success counts as settled. A failed fill does not trigger delivery, does not count as paid, and does not disappear from the record. Clearo keeps tracking it until it resolves or refunds.

Signed and verifiable

Every status comes from Relay's record, and every webhook Clearo sends is signed. Both sides of a settlement work from the same state, and either side can check it.

A refund address is required

No invoice can be paid until a refund address is set. If a payment fails, there is always a known address for the money to go back to — set before any money moves, not negotiated after.

Follow the payment instructions exactly

Paying with the wrong token can lose the money — a deposit in the wrong token may be unrecoverable. Send the exact token, on the exact chain, to the exact address on the invoice. Never send from ChangeNOW, FixedFloat, HTX, CoinEx or Tornado.cash — payments from those platforms are blocked and will not settle.

Questions, answered plainly

Which chains are supported?

USDC on Base and BNB Chain at launch. Every invoice states the exact token contract and the exact chain, so a paying agent never has to guess which USDC on which network.

What does “final” mean?

It means Relay reports the settlement as a success and the seller has received the USDC. That is the only moment Clearo notifies the seller and the order is released. Final means final — nothing before it counts as paid.

What happens if a buyer underpays?

An invoice settles only when the full amount arrives and reaches final. A short payment does not trigger delivery. The payment stays tracked against the invoice, and the refund address set before payment is where money goes back if the settlement cannot complete.

What if a payment arrives late, after the invoice expires?

Invoices expire 24 hours after they are issued. A payment sent after expiry is not treated as a settlement and does not release an order; it is returned to the refund address recorded on the invoice.

Who operates Clearo?

Clearo is run by an independent operator, not by Relay. The operator issues invoices, watches settlement status, verifies fees against quotes, and reconciles every settlement daily. Relay provides the cross-chain execution underneath.

How is Clearo different from Relay?

Relay moves the money across chains. Clearo is the layer on top that agents actually work with: invoice objects a program can parse and pay, a one-time deposit address per invoice, fee verification, and one webhook the moment payment is final. Pay from any chain. Get USDC on yours.
