Selling through Clearo

Clearo settles your sales across chains. You agree a job with a buyer, Clearo issues the invoice, the buyer pays from whatever chain its money sits on, and Clearo tells you the moment the payment is final on your chain. Then you deliver. Clearo never holds your money or your keys.

What you need

  • Your seller profile: your seller id, display name, receiving wallet, and the chain you receive USDC on (Base or BNB Chain at launch).
  • Your seller token: issued by the Clearo operator. Send it as the X-Clearo-Token header when creating invoices. A token only creates invoices under its own seller id.
  • The API address: https://clearo-i0gr.onrender.com. The service runs on a free hosting tier and may take up to a minute to answer after an idle period.

Your wallet keys never leave your system: buyers pay Relay's deposit address, and Relay delivers to your wallet directly.

The two touches

There are only two integration points.

1. Job agreed: create an invoice. Send POST /invoices with your seller profile, the job reference, the exact USDC amount, the buyer's refund address, and the chain the buyer pays from. Clearo returns the payment instructions: exact token contract, exact chain, exact amount to send, and a one-time deposit address. Hand those to the buyer exactly as issued.

FieldWhat it is
sellerYour profile: id, name, wallet, receive_chain_id.
job_refYour reference for the agreed job. It ties the payment to the work.
amount_usdcThe exact USDC amount the seller receives.
buyer_refund_addressRequired. The buyer's address on the chain it pays from. If a payment cannot complete, Relay returns it here automatically, minus gas. No refund address, no invoice.
pay_from_chain_idThe chain the buyer pays from, for example 8453 for Base or 56 for BNB Chain.
buyer_idOptional. The buyer agent's id, when known.

2. Payment final: deliver. Read GET /invoices/<id>. Deliver only when the status is success.

The quickstart

The whole integration is one copyable Python file, standard library only: seller_quickstart.py in the Clearo repository. Run it against the live API like this:

Terminal
export CLEARO_API_BASE=https://clearo-i0gr.onrender.com
export CLEARO_SELLER_TOKEN=<the token Clearo issued you>
export CLEARO_SELLER_ID=<your seller id>
export CLEARO_SELLER_WALLET=<your receiving wallet>
export CLEARO_SELLER_RECEIVE_CHAIN=8453

python seller_quickstart.py create --job-ref job-123 --amount 12.50 \
    --buyer-refund-address 0xBuyerRefund... --pay-from-chain 56
python seller_quickstart.py status --id CLR-20261114-XXXXXX

Creating an invoice returns the payment instructions to hand to the buyer. Poll the status; when it reads success the payment is final and you deliver.

Statuses

Every invoice carries one of these statuses. Only success and refund are final.

waitingNot finalThe invoice is issued and its deposit address is ready. No payment seen yet.
depositingNot finalA payment has been seen arriving and is being confirmed on the buyer's chain.
pendingNot finalThe deposit is confirmed and the cross-chain move is being prepared.
submittedNot finalThe fill has been submitted on your chain and is awaiting final confirmation.
successFinalYou have received the USDC. This is the only status that settles. Deliver now.
failureNot finalThe fill did not complete. This never means lost and is never a reason to deliver; the invoice stays open and watched.
refundFinalThe money has been returned to the refund address. The invoice is closed, not settled.

The rules that protect you

  • One invoice, one job, one deposit address. Never reuse an address.
  • The buyer must state a refund address before instructions are issued.
  • Clearo's fee is 25 bps of the invoice amount, printed on the invoice itself. No hidden charges, no minimum invoice.
  • Check the invoice before delivering: the amount and job reference are in the invoice record, and only a success status settles.
  • Paying with the wrong token can lose the money. The instructions name the exact token contract and chain for this reason.

Going live

Run the quickstart against the Clearo endpoint, issue a first invoice for a small amount, pay it from a second wallet you control, and watch it settle end to end. That dry run is the whole onboarding.
